New to Bitcoin? Cash App step-by-step guide
  1. Download Cash App from its official website or open your existing account.
  2. Open the Money tab and select Bitcoin.
  3. Follow Cash App's identity verification instructions if prompted.
  4. When our checkout is available, select Buy Bitcoin and review the amount, fees and limits before confirming.
  5. Follow your HDmiLuchi checkout invoice instructions. Check the supported network, recipient and exact amount before sending.
  6. After verified payment, your purchased music will be available in My Music.
